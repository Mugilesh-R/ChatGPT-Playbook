The Better Prompt Formula

A free quick-reference card from the ChatGPT Business Cheat Sheet & Prompt Playbook — the complete AI reference guide for freelancers, entrepreneurs, and content creators.

The quality of what you get from ChatGPT depends almost entirely on the quality of what you ask. This formula gives you a simple, reusable structure for writing prompts that produce useful, specific results — consistently.

R

Role

Tell ChatGPT what expertise or perspective to adopt. This shapes the tone, vocabulary, and angle of every response.

e.g. "Act as an experienced freelance copywriter specialising in B2B service businesses."
T

Task

State precisely what you want it to do. Use specific action verbs: write, compare, summarise, draft, rewrite, analyse.

e.g. "Write three subject line options for a cold outreach email."
C

Context

Provide the background it needs — about your business, the situation, or the materials you're working with.

e.g. "My business offers virtual bookkeeping to freelance designers with 1–5 clients."
A

Audience

Describe who will read or use the output. Role, industry, and knowledge level all help shape the response.

e.g. "The audience is a solo designer with no accounting background."
R

Requirements

Set constraints: tone, format, word count, language level, things to include or avoid.

e.g. "Plain language. No jargon. Keep each subject line under 8 words."
E

Examples

When relevant, show what a good result looks like. Even one example significantly improves output quality.

e.g. "I liked this style: 'Your books, sorted. No spreadsheets needed.'"
R

Review

After receiving the response, check it carefully and ask for targeted improvements where needed.

e.g. "Please revise this to be more conversational and under 150 words."

See it in action

Before — vague

Write me an email about my service.

After — structured

Act as a professional copywriter. Write a 150-word introduction email for [Business Name], a [Business Type] that helps [Target Customer] with [Core Service]. Addressed to [Recipient Type]. Tone: warm and professional. End with one clear call to action: [Desired Next Step].

3 things to know

1

Not every element is required.

Short tasks may need only Role + Task. Complex tasks benefit from all seven.

2

Always replace [placeholders].

A prompt with unfilled brackets produces a generic response. Fill every one with your real details.

3

Review before you use.

AI output can contain errors. Fact-check claims, confirm tone, and edit before publishing or sending.
